Good To Know

What affects your price.

We list starting prices for everything, and confirm the exact cost during your free consultation — before any work begins.

Hair length & density

Longer or thicker hair requires more product and time.

Skin condition

Specific concerns may need a targeted, higher-grade treatment.

Product choice

Premium brands like Kérastase & Olaplex cost more but last longer.

Senior stylist vs. junior

Senior stylists charge a small premium for their experience.
