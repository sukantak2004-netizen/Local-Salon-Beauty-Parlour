Before You Visit

Common questions.

A few quick answers to make your first visit smoother. Still curious? Just ask.

"No question is too small."
— The Lumiere team
Do I need to book in advance?

For regular services, we recommend booking 2–3 days ahead. For bridal and weekend slots, please book 2–3 weeks in advance. Walk-ins are welcome if we have a slot free, but the safest option is always to book.

How long does a typical appointment take?

A haircut takes 45–60 minutes. A facial is 60–75 minutes. Colour and keratin treatments take 2–3 hours depending on hair length. Bridal makeup takes about 2 hours. When you book, we'll confirm an accurate time slot.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when we have a slot free. Weekday mornings are usually your best bet for walk-ins. To avoid waiting, we recommend calling ahead — even 30 minutes' notice helps.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Same-day cancellations may be subject to a small fee, especially for long appointments like colour or bridal.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes — we use vegan, sulfate-free, paraben-free products from brands like Kérastase, Olaplex, O3+, and Casmara. If you have specific allergies or sensitivities, let us know at booking and we'll select the gentlest option.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, physical and digital gift cards from ₹1,000 upwards. Perfect for birthdays, thank-yous, or self-care nudges. Ask us at the front desk, by phone, or via WhatsApp.

Is the salon accessible?

Our ground-floor studio is wheelchair-accessible. If you have specific mobility needs, let us know when you book and we'll make arrangements in advance.
