Ask Us Anything
Do I need to book in advance?

Yes — we recommend 2–3 days ahead. For bridal, 2–3 weeks.

What products do you use?

Vegan, cruelty-free, sulfate-free brands like Kérastase, Olaplex, O3+, and Casmara.

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes — from ₹15,000 with trial, wedding day hair & makeup, and pre-bridal prep.

Can I reschedule?

Yes with 24 hours' notice. Same-day changes may incur a small fee.
