Tip Library

Bite-sized beauty notes.

Filter by category, or browse everything.

Hair

Wash your scalp, not your ends

Shampoo is for your scalp. Focus there, let the lather rinse through your ends. Conditioner goes mid-length to tips only.

Skin

SPF is non-negotiable

Even indoors. Even in monsoon. Even if your foundation has SPF 15 — it's not enough. Wear a dedicated SPF 30+ every single day.

Makeup

Less is more on humid days

Skip heavy foundation in humidity. A tinted moisturizer, cream blush, and waterproof mascara will last longer and look fresher.

Wellness

Sleep on silk, not cotton

Silk pillowcases reduce friction — less hair breakage, fewer sleep creases, and your skin stays hydrated. Small change, big difference.

DIY

Honey & yogurt face mask

Mix 1 tbsp raw honey + 1 tbsp plain yogurt + a pinch of turmeric. Apply for 12 minutes, rinse with cool water. Instant glow.

Hair

Oil before you wash

Apply warm coconut or almond oil 30 minutes before shampooing. It protects your lengths from drying out during washing.

Skin

Ice your face, gently

Wrap an ice cube in a soft cloth, glide over your face for 30 seconds in the morning. Depuffs, tightens pores, wakes you up.

Makeup

Set with a damp sponge

After powdering, press a damp beauty sponge over your T-zone. It melts powder into the skin for a natural, non-cakey finish.

Wellness

Hydrate from the inside

No serum replaces drinking water. Aim for 2.5–3L a day. Add lemon, mint, or cucumber if plain water bores you.

Featured Read · 6 min

The case for doing less to your hair.

A long-form note from our founder on why the healthiest hair is usually the simplest to care for.

LM
Lea Moreau
Founder & Lead Stylist · 5 November 2026

I've spent fifteen years around hair — thousands of heads, hundreds of colours, and probably a small mountain of product bottles in between. And if there's one lesson that keeps proving itself, it's this: the healthiest hair is usually the hair you do the least to.

Sounds counterintuitive for someone who runs a salon, I know. But hear me out.

Hot tools are the biggest culprit

Daily straightening or curling at high heat weakens the hair's protein structure over time. You won't see the damage immediately — the cuticle slowly lifts, moisture escapes, and one day you wake up with hair that feels brittle no matter how much serum you layer on.

If you can't skip the straightener, keep it below 180°C, use a heat protectant every single time, and give your hair two days off per week.

Stop over-washing

Unless you have extremely oily roots or work out daily, your scalp does not need a daily wash. Two to three times a week is plenty for most people. In between, use dry shampoo or simply a quick water rinse.

Use less product, not more

A common mistake is layering serum, oil, leave-in cream, and heat protectant every single day. They build up, weigh hair down, and actually clog the scalp. Pick one — the one your hair actually needs — and stick to it.

The simple routine I recommend

  • Oil your scalp and lengths 30 minutes before washing (once a week).
  • Wash with a gentle sulfate-free shampoo, focusing on the scalp.
  • Conditioner only mid-length to ends, leave 3 minutes, rinse cool.
  • Wrap in a soft cotton t-shirt — never rub with a rough towel.
  • Air-dry whenever possible. If you must blow-dry, use low heat.
  • Trim every 8–10 weeks to keep split ends from travelling up.

That's it. No ten-step routine. No expensive miracle cure. Just consistency, gentleness, and a little patience.

Come see us if you're dealing with real damage — we can fix a lot. But the best thing you can do for your hair is often the thing nobody sells: do less, but do it well.

Slow beauty, always.

DIY At Home

Three masks from your kitchen.

Simple, safe, and gentle. Always patch-test first, and skip if you have sensitive skin.

01

Honey + Yogurt Glow Mask

For dull skin
  • 1 tbsp raw honey
  • 1 tbsp plain yogurt
  • Pinch of turmeric (optional)

Mix into a smooth paste. Apply to clean skin, leave 12 minutes, rinse with cool water.

15 min · Weekly
02

Coffee Scalp Scrub

For hair growth
  • 2 tbsp ground coffee
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 tsp brown sugar

Massage gently into scalp for 5 minutes before washing. Stimulates circulation & removes buildup.

10 min · Biweekly
03

Rice Water Rinse

For shine & strength
  • ½ cup rice
  • 2 cups water
  • Few drops of rosemary oil

Soak rice 30 min, strain. Use the milky water as a final rinse after conditioner. Don't rinse out.

40 min · Weekly
